One theme, three modes

The look of the portfolio, ready to reuse on the blog, the GeoFM site and the Quantum papers site. Light is Editorial. Dark is Glass on laptops and tablets, and Bold on phones.

1. Load the fonts 2. Add theme.css 3. Toggle html.dark, or pin a mode with data-variant

Type

  • Body text is always Geist, 16px, line-height 1.6.
  • Editorial headings use Instrument Serif 400, with the key phrase in teal italic.
  • Glass headings use Geist 500 with tight tracking (−0.035em). The accent phrase gets a teal gradient.
  • Bold headings use Geist 600, tracking −0.045em, big and short.

Colour

  • One accent family: deep teal #0E6B66 on light, mint #7FD1C6 on Glass, #4FB3A9 on Bold.
  • Text steps: ink for headings, i2 for body, mu for dates and captions.
  • Use tokens only (var(--ac), var(--ln)…), so one class works in all three modes.

Surfaces

  • Editorial: white cards, hairline border, very soft shadow, 14px radius.
  • Glass: 5% white, 22px backdrop blur, 20px radius, teal and blue glow behind the page.
  • Bold: flat #121416 cards, 8px radius, teal footer band.

Motion

  • Ease out cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1); cards lift 4px, rows nudge right.
  • The headline phrase rotates every few seconds; it fades in Editorial and slides in Bold.
  • Everything respects reduced motion.
